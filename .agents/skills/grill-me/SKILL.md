# Grill Me

Read project/PROJECT.md and any existing decisions. Ask one concise, high-value question at a time about users, outcomes, constraints, examples and failure modes. Do not ask the same question twice. Push back on contradictory requirements respectfully. Summarize emerging understanding without substituting assumptions for decisions. Exit when the owner can approve a plain-English problem statement and the smallest useful outcome. Save resulting clarifications in project/PROJECT.md and update HANDOFF.md. Do not code in this stage.

## Completion contract

Show **what was observed**, **what changed**, **what is unverified**, and **what the owner decides next**. Follow `AGENTS.md` and `docs/WORKFLOW.md`; neither skills nor role names grant extra permissions.
